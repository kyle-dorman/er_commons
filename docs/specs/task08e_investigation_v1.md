## Gate changes and remaining human authority

Ready plus confirmed links and explicitly unresolved question gaps is a valid
investigation input under `task08d.investigation.v1`. Do not demand resolved
scope as a precondition. Supporting targets may be supporting claims; follow the
approved acyclic version-bound chain to a direct claim. Preserve all intermediate
claims, rationales, exact question text and target versions. Reject stale, cyclic,
dangling or unapproved relationships rather than silently flattening the chain.

Evidence judgments are independent of response-claim approval. Support, partial
support, context, contradiction, no support found and unresolved outcomes all
remain possible. A valid citation and a technically consistent review are not
eligibility or answer adequacy. A partial or conflicting evidence record may be
saved and reviewed without being upgraded to fully supported. Human eligibility
can stop or hold a case; no algorithm resolves its question gaps or decides its
semantic adequacy. Final advancement requires explicit version-bound human
evidence and eligibility decisions; old unresolved scope is never silently erased.

## Bounded search and canonical evidence

Use [prompt v2](../prompts/task08e_claim_citation_search_v2.md) and the general
mechanics of [07D search](task07d_search_protocol_v1.md): three baseline query
purposes per claim, at most three queries and ten hits each, at least one
full-corpus query, and a shared six-query follow-up allowance per case. Shared
queries execute once but debit every served claim. Named gap-driven revisions
must precede additional queries. No stop merely because apparent support appears.

Resolve every supplied reference separately from whether it supports the claim.
Inspect complete sections/tables and retain canonical source/extraction/edition,
ordered spans, physical/printed pages, notes, captions, family membership and
source-usability warnings. No source-count cap. Every hit receives a retained,
rejected, duplicate or unresolved disposition. Preserve unsuccessful searches,
truncation, budget stops and unestablished claims without asserting corpus-wide
absence. No new calculation or CSV tool is implied by a health-risk label.

Use only currently eligible accepted source material. Response text is context
and leads, not evidence. Later electrification assertions or external references
remain provenance/limits; do not obtain new external evidence to force support.
Any inherited Final F1 exception retains actual edition and accepted limits.
Historical source-bound boundary overrides require fresh explicit review rather
than automatic reuse. No source correction or re-extraction is authorized here.

## Native packet storage revision

`task08e.packet_reference.v1` keeps full canonical packets in immutable external
files and sends the review application exact relative packet references instead
of duplicating each case packet in every claim task. This reduces browser payload
without shortening any visible source unit, warning, proposed summary or control.
The loader verifies raw file SHA-256, semantic packet identity and the original
expanded task identity, with root containment. Export first verifies exact native
task/project/form/reviewer bindings, then hydrates temporary copies for maintained
normalization; records retain native and expanded identities. Packet caching lasts
only for one export call, so later exports revalidate source bytes.

The initial full-packet live project is diagnostic: repeated browser crashes and
stalls prevented readiness despite valid blank native exports. Preserve it and
qualify the storage revision with separate source-free queue/save/edit/export
fixtures before publishing a fresh replacement. No saved human evidence decisions
are migrated or inferred.
