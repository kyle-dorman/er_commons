## Version and source contract

The shared preparation version is `task08e.evidence_review.v1`; the search prompt
is `task08e.claim_citation_search.v1`. Version each schema, generated form and
saved decision/export independently and bind actual bytes by SHA-256. Preserve
model/prompt/input/output provenance for future proposals; Astra
(`gpt-6-astra`) is the proposal model, never the eligibility reviewer. No model
proposal run occurs during source-free preparation.

The live loader must verify accepted 08B selection, exact frozen 08C question,
accepted 08D coordination and completed export, current approved direct/supporting
claims and relationships, complete linked responses, inventory, extraction,
usability and source-edition identities. It must compare exact selected membership
against the two authorized comments. File recency, saved-annotation counts,
claim approvals within an incomplete case and chat completion statements cannot
release claims. Stops/unresolved cases remain accounted for but cannot enter
search. Recheck this gate immediately before future execution.

Use packet-v2 complete canonical section/table records. Preserve source URL,
license/retrieval reference, actual edition, extraction version, physical and
printed pages, ordered anchors, complete canonical snapshots, quoted spans,
limitations and discovery provenance. Source hashes and accepted-corpus membership
are separate checks. A matching quote alone is insufficient. Original responses
are curator context/search leads, not evidence. Inherited Final F1 substitution
may enter only if explicitly bound by the current accepted source contract, with
its actual edition and limitations; no general Final-EIR permission follows.

## Bounded discovery rules (inactive until released)

Before a search, bind a case plan and full eligible-corpus FTS5 index. Reuse the
maintained [07D protocol](task07d_search_protocol_v1.md)'s general mechanics only:
`unicode61`, default `bm25()`, ascending score then source/record ID, unchanged
corpus statistics for filtered queries, and exact runtime/index hashes.

- Resolve each distinct supplied reference; retain original and actual target,
  ambiguities and corrections. Inspect attached notes/tables and full units.
- Cover proposition, assumptions/method/baseline, and possible contradictions or
  limiting conditions. At most three baseline queries per approved claim, ten
  ranked hits each, with at least one query over the full eligible corpus.
  Shared queries execute once and debit each motivating claim's allocation.
- Inspect every returned hit; retain retained/rejected/duplicate/unresolved
  dispositions and reasons. Preserve truncated counts and syntax failures.
- Gaps may trigger six additional successful queries per case, ten hits each.
  Record gap, parent receipt and changed terms before each. The lead owns this
  shared budget; agents do not each receive six extra queries.
- Follow only accepted resolved links: one hop initially, at most two after a
  recorded expansion. Unresolved references are lexical leads, never graph edges.
- Stop after purpose coverage and applicable follow-ups, or with an explicit
  budget/technical stop. Further work needs a named plan revision before execution.
  A bounded miss is not corpus-wide absence or proof of insufficient evidence.

Deduplicate complete units by source/extraction/kind/canonical ID while retaining
all query/reference associations. Keep tables distinct from enclosing sections.
No small citation-count cap; order questioned/core passages first and preserve
counterevidence, qualifications and all pending candidates. Save full citable
text plus a focused, editable source summary; summaries never replace anchors.
Prompt rendering fails on unresolved placeholders or missing bound inputs.

## Human controls and export gates

Reuse the maintained evidence form's explicit per-claim/source relationship,
relationship note, overall outcome and outcome note. The 08E wrapper adds editable
curator summaries; an unresolved outcome preserves held work. No human choice is
preselected. Preserve submitted annotations, drafts,
notes, edited summaries and their source identities independently. Never infer
support from direct/supporting claim relevance or from selecting a candidate.

Case eligibility is a separate explicit human decision: advance, stop or
unresolved, with reason, reviewer, timestamp and exact reviewed input versions.
The review must present original comment, frozen question, complete response,
evidence, gaps, usability and relevant references. It cannot be finalized for
advancement before the exact evidence decisions it reviews are bound. Missing
or stale decisions stay held, not automatically excluded.

For eventual advancement the validator must enforce:

- Exact imported task/annotation/project/reviewer identity, unchanged displayed
  data and active form bytes; reject foreign/duplicate/inactive controls, extra
  tasks, empty approvals and stale versions. Preserve native exports verbatim.
- Every expected claim and relationship accounted for; unresolved, partial or
  conflicting reviews cannot silently become full support. Structural failures
  are hard errors; human unresolved work remains exportable as held diagnostics.
- Canonical source closure and unchanged approved claims/questions. A proposed
  change returns to the owning stage and invalidates affected downstream review.
- Deduplicated case support/context lists retain all relationship history;
  support wins over context only across explicitly reviewed uses. Context-only
  sources never satisfy required support. Rejected/contradictory candidates and
  search gaps remain in the audit. Partial-only citation use needs a fresh human
  disposition; never silently map it to support or context.
- Explicit human eligibility on the current evidence version, separate from 08B
  selection. Material later-revision, new Final-EIR analysis, external evidence,
  mitigation-change, unusable-text and unavailable-visual dependencies are for
  human exclusion review. Required tables need canonical text/structure and
  render-verified usability for every required family member. No CSV tool or new
  calculation is authorized by the health-risk group name.
- A qualification namespace always yields zero advancing cases/claims and no
  allowed next stage, even when simulated controls say advance. Fresh no-clobber
  outputs and a completion manifest written last distinguish complete exports
  from interrupted work. Qualification cannot become live by changing a flag.

`task_08/qualification/`, separate from `health_risk/08e/`. Use distinct fixture
identities for queue navigation, long-text/summary edits, shared sources with
independent roles, canonical sections/tables, zero-candidate gaps, unresolved
work, explicit stop and simulated advance. Exercise actual installed Label Studio
queue submission, reload and native export, not only generated XML or mock JSON.
Tests should challenge changed source anchors, version/task/control tampering,
missing decisions and accidental promotion. A simulated reviewer is never Kyle's
semantic approval. Persist project IDs, snapshots, screenshots, native exports,
validation and independent reviews. Report any unqualified feature explicitly.

One bounded reviewer checks source/anchor and historical-decision isolation;
another checks UI/export semantics. The lead integrates findings and inspects
maintainability. These technical reviews do not decide evidence or eligibility.

## Research and learning

Checked 2026-09-30: maintained Label Studio guidance for
[configuration](https://labelstud.io/guide/setup),
[task imports](https://labelstud.io/guide/tasks), and
[native export](https://labelstud.io/guide/export), plus
[SQLite FTS5](https://www.sqlite.org/fts5.html).
Native controls and separate imported task data fit a reusable shared form;
model predictions and saved annotations are distinct. Actual save/export checks
are needed because a visible value need not be a saved human decision. FTS5
receipts preserve reproducible lexical retrieval, not a completeness guarantee.
Stable canonical identities and separate relationship decisions prevent a correct
citation from being mistaken for support. A narrow adapter retains the maintained
07D validation without carrying its case-specific acceptance history forward.
