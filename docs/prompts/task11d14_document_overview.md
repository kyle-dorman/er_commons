The supplied passages come from the Draft Environmental Impact Report (EIR) and accompanying appendices for the proposed Baylands development in Brisbane, San Mateo County. Prepared under the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA), this document set describes the project, evaluates its potential environmental effects and identifies mitigation measures. It represents the draft EIR circulated for public and agency review and comment.

The main EIR presents the environmental analysis. Its appendices provide the proposed Specific Plan, technical studies, supporting plans and approval records. All supplied passages belong to this project’s environmental-review document set.
