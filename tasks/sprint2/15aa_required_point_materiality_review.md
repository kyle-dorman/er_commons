detention replacement mechanism remains relevant even though the model puts it
only in support. Distinguish judge equivalence errors from label-policy issues.

and prepares an ordered discussion queue. Show exact current point, proposed
wording or optional move, evidence and tradeoff. Do not claim the adaptive review
is independent validation or that higher pass rates demonstrate better labels.
